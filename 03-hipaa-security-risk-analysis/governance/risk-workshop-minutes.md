## Summary
Clinical, IT, Clinical Engineering, Privacy, Compliance, and Legal identified threats and vulnerabilities to ePHI across the 68 in-scope systems, the 6,120-device fleet, and the business-associate footprint. Participants worked from the Phase 02 inventory and data-flow maps so that every risk traces to a specific ePHI asset or flow.

Particular attention went to threats with **patient-safety** consequences — ransomware-driven care disruption, medical-device compromise, and integrity failures in orders or results — which the scoring model treats as capable of elevating a risk beyond pure confidentiality impact.
