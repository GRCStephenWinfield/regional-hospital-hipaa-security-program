## Summary
The Security Official presented the completed **§164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A) risk analysis**: **56 risks** identified across the 68 ePHI systems — **11 High, 27 Moderate, 18 Low** — with an overall posture of **Moderate**. The Committee noted that an incomplete or missing risk analysis is the most frequently cited failure in OCR enforcement, and that MercyBridge's analysis is now documented, scoped to the full ePHI estate, and subject to periodic review.

The Committee reviewed the 11 High risks and the associated **risk management plan** (§164.308(a)(1)(ii)(B)), and approved time-bounded exceptions for the Wave 2 and Wave 3 items — fleet-wide encryption, network re-architecture, and medical-device lifecycle work cannot complete within 90 days in a 24×7 clinical environment — with named interim compensating controls.

## Decisions
- DEC-303 accept the register at 56 risks; DEC-304 approve Wave 2–3 time-bounded exceptions.
