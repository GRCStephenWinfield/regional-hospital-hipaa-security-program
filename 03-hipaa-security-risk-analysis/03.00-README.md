## What this phase is

This is the **cornerstone of the entire HIPAA Security Program**. Section **164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A)** requires a covered entity to "conduct an accurate and thorough assessment of the potential risks and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic protected health information." An absent, incomplete, or stale risk analysis is the **single most frequently cited failure in OCR enforcement** — it appears in resolution agreement after resolution agreement.

This phase delivers that analysis across all **68 ePHI systems** identified in Phase 02, following **NIST SP 800-66 Rev. 2** and **NIST SP 800-30**: threat identification, vulnerability identification, assessment of security measures already in place, likelihood and impact determination using healthcare-specific criteria, and the resulting **risk register of 56 risks**. It then delivers the separate-but-related **§164.308(a)(1)(ii)(B) risk management plan**, the formal report to the Board, and the periodic-review process that keeps the analysis a living document.

## A note on two distinct requirements

Practitioners frequently conflate them, and OCR cites them separately:

- **§164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A) — Risk Analysis:** *identify* the risks. Delivered in 03.01–03.07 and 03.09.
- **§164.308(a)(1)(ii)(B) — Risk Management:** *implement security measures sufficient to reduce those risks to a reasonable and appropriate level.* Established in 03.08 and executed through the safeguards in Phases 04–07.
