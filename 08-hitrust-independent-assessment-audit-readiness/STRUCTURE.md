## Conventions
`NN.NN` numbering in execution order; metadata block per document; footer navigation; Mermaid diagrams; color-coded Excel. ADRs are numbered continuously across the portfolio (Phase 08 uses 0019–0021). The keystone deliverables are the penetration test results (08.03), the HITRUST i1 assessment results (08.07), the OCR Audit Protocol readiness review (08.08), and the control-to-risk traceability (08.11) in which two risks are **raised** on independent evidence. The Excel trackers are generated by parsing the narrative documents — the pen test findings are extracted from 08.03 and the risk movements from 08.11 — so the workbooks cannot drift from the prose.
