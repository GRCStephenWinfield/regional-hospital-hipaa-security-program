## Summary
Beacon Assurance confirmed the **i1 Validated Assessment** result: **93.1% implementation**, **174 of 182** requirement statements at or above threshold, **zero Non-Compliant**, and **eight corrective action plans**. Certification runs **2026-11-30 to 2027-11-29**.

The Security Official recorded an important caveat for the record, and it appears in the report itself: **HITRUST certification is not a determination of HIPAA compliance by OCR.** OCR certifies no one, and no external framework substitutes for the Security Rule's own requirements. HITRUST provides structured, third-party-validated assurance that partners and payers increasingly ask for — that is its value, and its limit.

Five of the eight CAPs converge with Internal Audit recommendations, which the Committee treated as corroboration rather than duplication.
