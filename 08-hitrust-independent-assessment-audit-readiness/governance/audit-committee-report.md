## Summary
The Committee received the results of four independent lenses applied to the security program.

**Internal Audit** issued a **Satisfactory** opinion with nine recommendations, none Critical or High. **Ironwood Security Labs** identified **16 findings** in the penetration test — three High — **all remediated and retested**. **Beacon Assurance** validated the **HITRUST i1 assessment**, certified at **93.1% implementation** with zero Non-Compliant requirements and eight corrective action plans. OCR Audit Protocol readiness mapped 91 in-scope inquiries and raised **no new findings**.

The Committee noted with approval two items that ran against the grain of a favourable report: **R-41 was raised** because pen-test finding PT-03 disproved an assumption about asset-inventory completeness, and the **R-23 conditional residual lapsed** back to its prior rating because the joint restoration test with the EHR vendor is overdue. Management was directed to complete that test as a priority.

## Decisions
- DEC-803 raise R-41 on evidence; DEC-804 lapse the R-23 conditional residual.
