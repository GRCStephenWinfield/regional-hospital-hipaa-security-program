## Summary
Reviewed the enterprise discovery results: **210 information systems**, of which **68 create, receive, maintain, or transmit ePHI**. Confirmed the criticality tiering (12 Critical / 21 High / 24 Moderate / 11 Low) and the exclusion rationale for the remaining 142 systems. Noted that the 2025 NPRM would make a formal asset inventory and network map mandatory — MercyBridge now satisfies that in advance.
