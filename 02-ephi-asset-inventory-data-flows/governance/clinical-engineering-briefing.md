## Summary
Reviewed the **6,120-device fleet**, of which **3,170 hold or transmit ePHI** and **1,300 run unsupported operating systems**. MDS2 documentation is on file for 4,284 devices. Agreed that network segmentation and compensating controls — not patching alone — are the primary safeguard strategy for legacy devices, and that joint Clinical Engineering / Information Security ownership will be formalized.
