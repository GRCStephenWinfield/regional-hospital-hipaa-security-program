## Purpose

**45 CFR §164.308(a)(3)(i)** requires policies and procedures to ensure that all members of the workforce **have appropriate access to ePHI**, and to **prevent those workforce members who do not have access from obtaining access**. Both halves matter: the standard is as concerned with the clinician who cannot reach a chart at 0300 as with the leaver whose account is still live.

This document delivers the standard's three implementation specifications for MercyBridge's **~6,500 workforce members** across 4 hospitals and ~30 outpatient sites, and defines the **joiner–mover–leaver (JML)** lifecycle that governs employed staff, credentialed medical staff, residents, students, contractors and volunteers alike.

> **HIPAA's definition of "workforce" (§160.103) is broader than "employee": it includes volunteers, trainees and other persons whose conduct is under the direct control of the entity, whether or not they are paid. MercyBridge's JML lifecycle therefore covers all 6,500, not just the payroll population.**

## 1. The Three Implementation Specifications

| Specification | Citation | R / A | Requirement | MercyBridge determination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Authorization and/or supervision | §164.308(a)(3)(ii)(A) | **Addressable** | Authorise and/or supervise workforce members who work with ePHI or in locations where it may be accessed | **Reasonable and appropriate — implemented.** Manager authorisation for every access grant; supervision model for students, volunteers and contractors |
| Workforce clearance procedure | §164.308(a)(3)(ii)(B) | **Addressable** | Determine that a workforce member's access to ePHI is appropriate | **Reasonable and appropriate — implemented.** Tiered pre-access screening, licensure/credential verification, exclusion checks |
| Termination procedures | §164.308(a)(3)(ii)(C) | **Addressable** | Terminate access when employment ends or when access is no longer appropriate | **Reasonable and appropriate — implemented.** SLA-bound revocation with automated triggers and exception reporting |

All three are addressable and all three are implemented; no §164.306(d)(3)(ii) rationale is on file. Under the **2025 NPRM** all three would become mandatory, with an explicit **24-hour termination deadline** — MercyBridge has adopted a **4-hour SSO / 24-hour enterprise** standard, ahead of the proposal.

|---|---|---|---|---|

Every non-employee category carries a **mandatory expiry date** on its identity record. Employed staff records do not expire; every other record does, with a maximum of 12 months for contractors, the rotation end date for students, and the credentialing period for medical staff.

## 3. Workforce Clearance Procedure — §164.308(a)(3)(ii)(B)

Clearance answers a single question: *is this person's access to ePHI appropriate?* MercyBridge answers it before the first grant, and again at defined intervals.

| Tier | Applies to | Checks performed | Re-check cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 — Basic** | Volunteers; workforce with no ePHI access | Identity verification; reference check; OIG/GSA exclusion screening; confidentiality attestation | Annual exclusion screen |
| **T2 — Standard** | Employed staff and students with ePHI access | T1 + criminal background check; employment and education verification; state abuse/neglect registry where required | Exclusion monthly; background re-check every 3 years |
| **T3 — Enhanced** | Licensed clinicians, residents, contractors, privileged IT, HIM/coding, revenue cycle, security staff | T2 + primary-source licensure verification; DEA where applicable; sanction/board discipline check; credit check for finance-privileged roles; drug screen per role | Licensure at each renewal; exclusion monthly; background re-check every 2 years |
| **T4 — Privileged** | Domain and EHR administrators, database administrators, backup operators, break-glass approvers | T3 + enhanced screening, references from prior privileged roles, annual re-attestation, dual-approval on grant | Annual |

| Clearance metric | Position at 2026-07-15 |
|---|---|
| Workforce with a completed, current clearance on file | 6,461 of 6,500 (99.4%) |
| Records with an expired or missing clearance element | 39 — all volunteers or dormant student records; access suspended pending completion |
| Monthly OIG/GSA exclusion screening coverage | 6,500 of 6,500 (100%) |
| Exclusion hits in trailing 12 months | 2 — both pre-hire, offers withdrawn |
| Primary-source licensure verifications current | 795 of 795 clinicians and residents (100%) |
| T4 privileged population under annual re-attestation | 41 individuals |

**Exclusion screening is a compliance control with a security effect.** An excluded individual must not hold access; the monthly screen therefore feeds the identity governance platform directly, and a hit triggers immediate access suspension pending review.

## 4. Authorization and Supervision — §164.308(a)(3)(ii)(A)

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who authorises | The workforce member's **line manager** (employees), **department chair** (medical staff), **programme director** (residents), **named preceptor** (students), **named MercyBridge sponsor** (contractors), **Volunteer Services coordinator** (volunteers) |
| What is authorised | A **role**, not a set of individual entitlements — roles are defined in 04.05 and mapped to minimum necessary |
| Exception grants | Any entitlement outside the approved role requires a written business justification, IAM review and Information Security Manager approval; auto-expires at 90 days |
| Supervision | Students, volunteers and contractors may access ePHI only in furtherance of the supervised assignment; the supervisor attests quarterly that the assignment continues |
| Segregation | Requesting, approving and provisioning are performed by three different parties; no self-approval, including for the IAM team and the Security Official |
| Contractor identity | Contractors receive a visually distinct badge, a `x-` prefixed account, and a mandatory sponsor field that blocks provisioning if empty |

## 5. The Joiner–Mover–Leaver Lifecycle

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    subgraph JOIN["Joiner"]
    J1["Requisition / credentialing<br/>Workday · Medical Staff Affairs"] --> J2["Clearance tier T1-T4<br/>background · exclusion · licensure"]
    J2 --> J3["Manager authorises role<br/>no role = no access"]
    J3 --> J4["IAM provisions from role catalogue<br/>unique ID · MFA enrolment"]
    J4 --> J5["New-hire security training<br/>before first ePHI login"]
    end
    subgraph MOVE["Mover"]
    M1["Transfer · promotion · rotation<br/>HR or scheduling feed"] --> M2["Prior role revoked first<br/>then new role granted"]
    M2 --> M3["Manager attests within 5 days"]
    end
    subgraph LEAVE["Leaver"]
    L1["Termination · resignation<br/>rotation end · contract end"] --> L2["Automated disable<br/>SSO domain in 4 hours"]
    L2 --> L3["Non-SSO systems<br/>within 24 hours"]
    L3 --> L4["Badge · keys · devices · media returned"]
    L4 --> L5["Exit attestation<br/>confidentiality obligations survive"]
    end
    J5 --> M1
    M3 --> L1
    L5 --> AUD["Weekly exception report<br/>Marcus Reed · monthly to Steering Committee"]
```

**Accumulation is the failure mode.** The classic hospital problem is the mover, not the leaver: a nurse who moves from the ED to oncology and keeps both entitlement sets, then to case management and keeps all three. MercyBridge's mover rule is therefore **revoke-then-grant**, not grant-and-review, with a manager attestation five business days later confirming that the residual entitlement set is correct.

## 6. Termination Procedures — §164.308(a)(3)(ii)(C)

### 6.1 Triggers and Timeliness Standards

| Trigger | Source system | Revocation standard | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voluntary resignation | HR (Workday) | End of last shift, ≤ 4 hours after | Manager confirms last shift time |
| Involuntary termination | HR, expedited flag | **Immediate — before notification meeting** | IAM stages the disable; Security present if risk assessed |
| Death in service | HR | ≤ 24 hours | Handled with HR and Chaplaincy |
| Contract end / non-renewal | Vendor management + sponsor | Automatic at expiry date | Sponsor re-attests to extend |
| Rotation end (students, residents) | Scheduling / GME feed | Automatic at rotation end date | No manual step required |
| Medical staff resignation or non-reappointment | Medical Staff Affairs | ≤ 24 hours | Coordinated with credentialing |
| Volunteer disengagement | Volunteer Services | ≤ 24 hours | Badge return enforced |
| Leave of absence &gt; 30 days | HR | Suspend, not delete, at day 31 | Reinstated on return with manager confirmation |
| Exclusion-list hit | Monthly screen | **Immediate suspension** | Review before any reinstatement |
| Sanction T3/T4 | POL-03 panel | Immediate restriction or revocation | 04.02 |

### 6.2 The Baseline Problem and What Changed

Phase 03 registered **R-04**: departed or transferred workforce retained access beyond 24 hours **outside the Halcyon SSO domain**. The SSO domain was fine; the nine ePHI systems with local accounts were not.

| Measure | Baseline (trailing 12 months to 2026-03-31) | Position at 2026-07-15 | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leaver events processed | 1,412 | — | — |
| Median revocation time, SSO domain | 3.1 hours | **0.9 hours** (automated) | ≤ 4 hours |
| Median revocation time, non-SSO systems | 31 hours | **7.4 hours** | ≤ 24 hours |
| Leaver events fully revoked within 24 hours (all systems) | 92.4% | **98.1%** | ≥ 99.5% by 2026-10-31 |
| Events exceeding 24 hours | 107 | 12 (rolling quarter) | ≤ 5 |
| ePHI systems still requiring manual de-provisioning | 9 | **3** | 0 by 2026-12-31 |
| Orphaned accounts found at quarterly reconciliation | 84 | **6** | 0 |
| Badge deactivation within 24 hours | 96.0% | 99.3% | ≥ 99.5% |
| Devices and media recovered at exit | 91% | 97.6% | ≥ 98% |

**What changed:** HR and Medical Staff Affairs feeds were wired directly into the identity governance platform as authoritative sources; six of the nine manual systems were federated to SSO; a **daily** orphan-account reconciliation replaced the quarterly one; and unrecovered devices now trigger an automatic remote-wipe request and a sanction-panel referral at day 7.

### 6.3 Termination Checklist (Evidence Record)

| Step | Owner | SLA | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Trigger received from authoritative source | HR / MSA / Sponsor | Same day | Feed record |
| 2. SSO and directory account disabled | IAM (automated) | ≤ 4 hours | IAM audit log |
| 3. Non-SSO ePHI accounts disabled | IAM | ≤ 24 hours | Ticket + system log |

| 5. Remote access, VPN and MFA tokens revoked | IAM | ≤ 4 hours | VPN/MFA log |
| 6. Physical badge and keys deactivated | Security Services | ≤ 24 hours | Badge system log |
| 7. Devices, media and paper records returned | Manager | Exit day | Asset return form |

The completed checklist is a §164.316(b) record retained six years.
