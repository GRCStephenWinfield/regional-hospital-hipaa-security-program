## Summary
Exercised the **emergency mode operation plan** — the specification that matters most in a hospital, because when the EHR is unavailable, care does not stop. The exercise covered paper charting, downtime kits distributed across sites, the read-only clinical viewer, and manual blood-bank crossmatch procedures.

Confirmed Tier 1 recovery objectives (RTO ≤ 4 hours, RPO ≤ 15 minutes) and validated that ePHI custody is maintained during downtime — paper records generated in downtime are reconciled back into the EHR and are subject to the same safeguards.
