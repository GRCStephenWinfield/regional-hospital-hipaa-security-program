## Summary
The Committee approved **15 of the 24 HIPAA policies** covering the administrative safeguards, consolidating 41 legacy documents into a single governed suite. The remaining nine policies depend on technical decisions in Phase 05 and will be approved on the same cycle.

The Committee affirmed the position that **addressable does not mean optional** — MercyBridge implements every addressable specification rather than documenting alternatives, which also positions the organization for the 2025 NPRM's proposed removal of the distinction.

## Decisions
- DEC-401 implement all addressable specs; DEC-402 consolidate to a 24-policy suite.
