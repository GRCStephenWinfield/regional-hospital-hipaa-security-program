## Summary
Reviewed the joiner-mover-leaver lifecycle across the **6,500-member workforce**, including clinicians, residents, students, contractors, and volunteers. Termination access revocation within 24 hours improved from 92.4% to 98.1%.

The standing elevated EHR account population was reduced from **214 to 63** — 118 removed outright and 33 converted to just-in-time elevation — directly treating risk R-03. Break-the-glass emergency access now receives **100% post-hoc review**, up from 62%, which matters clinically: the control must never block emergency care, so the compensating control is review after the fact rather than prevention.

## Decisions
- DEC-403 reduce standing elevated access.
