## What this phase is

The administrative safeguards are the **largest of the Security Rule's three families** — nine standards and twenty-one implementation specifications covering how MercyBridge governs, staffs, trains, and organizes itself around the protection of ePHI. They are also where OCR looks first, because most breaches trace back to a process failure rather than a missing technology.

This phase implements all nine standards for the **68 ePHI systems** and **~6,500 workforce members**: the security management process (sanction policy and activity review), the Security Official in operation, workforce security and the joiner-mover-leaver lifecycle, information access management under the minimum-necessary principle, awareness and training, incident procedures, the contingency plan including hospital **emergency mode operation**, periodic evaluation, and business associate contracts. It also delivers **15 of the 24 policies** in the HIPAA policy suite.

## A position worth stating plainly

Eleven of the twenty-one specifications are marked **addressable**. That word is widely misread as *optional* — and skipping addressable specifications without documented analysis is one of the most common findings in OCR enforcement. MercyBridge **implements every addressable specification** rather than documenting alternatives. That is the stronger posture today, and it means no remediation scramble when the **2025 NPRM** removes the required/addressable distinction.
