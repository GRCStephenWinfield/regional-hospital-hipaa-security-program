## Summary
The Panel reviewed the three incidents investigated in the period, covering **3,961 individuals**, and reached a determination of **no reportable breach** in each case.

The Panel restated the governing principle: under §164.402 a breach is **presumed**, and §164.414(b) places the burden on MercyBridge to demonstrate a low probability of compromise. A balanced case is notified.

A nuance in **INC-2026-0031** was recorded deliberately: the lost laptop was encrypted and therefore safe-harboured — but the **printed clinic session list was not**. Paper PHI can never be safe-harboured by encryption, so the entire determination turned on the paper, not the device.
