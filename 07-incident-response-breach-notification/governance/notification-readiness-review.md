## Summary
Approved the notification instrument set **NT-01 … NT-14**, including the model individual notice carrying the five §164.404(c) required elements as fixed, non-deletable sections, the media notice outline, and the HHS submission checklist.

Confirmed the timing architecture: individuals **without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days** from discovery; HHS **contemporaneously for 500 or more** individuals and via the **annual log within 60 days of year end** below that threshold; **media notice where more than 500 residents of a single state** are affected. Notices are drafted to a Grade-8 readability standard with translation and accessibility obligations, and the call-centre vendor was tested to **250,000 notices in ten business days**.
