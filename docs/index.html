Where they converged

Three of five lenses landed on the same underlying defect working independently: enterprise-scale recoverability is not proven. The value of multiple lenses is in the overlap, and the overlap is the program's honest headline.

What this program does not claim

Claimed

  • Independently tested — 16 findings, every fix re-tested by the people who found it
  • Independently audited — Satisfactory opinion from an auditor reporting to the Board
  • Externally certified — HITRUST i1 at 93.1%, quality-reviewed by HITRUST
  • Regulator-ready — 8,412 evidence artefacts, median production 1.5 business days

Not claimed

  • HIPAA compliance. OCR neither endorses nor recognises any certification as a compliance determination
  • Enterprise-scale recoverability. The test that would prove it did not happen
  • Durability. Every remediation is under three months old; time is the only test
  • That it was only good. Three low-severity incidents in eleven months is also a favourable draw
