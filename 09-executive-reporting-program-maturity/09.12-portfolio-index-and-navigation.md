## Purpose

One hundred and eighteen documents across nine phases is a useful body of work and a hostile one to navigate. This is the index that fixes that.

It exists for three readers. **An auditor or regulator** who needs the document addressing a specific Security Rule citation and needs it in one hop. **An executive or hiring manager** who has fifteen minutes and wants to know whether the work is real. **An operational owner** who needs to find the procedure behind a control they now own.

Each gets a route. §3 is the complete index. §4 maps every standard and implementation specification in §164.308, §164.310, §164.312, §164.314 and §164.316 to the documents that address it. §5 and §6 are curated reading paths. §7 maps the questions a regulator actually asks to the document that answers them.
