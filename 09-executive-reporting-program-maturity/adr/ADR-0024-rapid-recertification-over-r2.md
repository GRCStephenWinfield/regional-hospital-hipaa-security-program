# ADR-0024 — Elect Rapid Recertification for 2027; Defer the r2 Decision to 2028

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | **Accepted** |
| Date | 2026-12-05 |
| Deciders | Daniel Cho (CISO), Karen Boyd (CCO), Anthony Ruiz (CIO) |
| Related | ADR-0020 (i1 over r2, Phase 08) |

## Context

The i1 certificate issued 2026-11-30 expires **2027-11-29**. Three paths exist for 2027: rapid recertification of the i1, a fresh full i1 validated assessment, or an escalation to r2.

## Decision

Elect **rapid recertification** for the 2027 cycle. Evaluate **r2 for the 2028 cycle** against written criteria, decided by 2027-12.

## Rationale

r2 assesses maturity across policy, procedure, implementation, measurement and management. At 2026-12-31 the programme meets **one** of the five criteria the organisation set for electing r2 — zero High risks. Operating history is under three months on most remediated controls, eight CAPs are open, and evidence automation sits at 64% against an 80% target. Electing r2 now would produce low maturity scores on controls that are in fact well implemented, because the *evidence period* does not exist.

Rapid recertification preserves certification continuity while that history accumulates. Its conditions are also, usefully, the same things the programme should be doing anyway: no material scope change, no significant control failure, and the corrective actions from the prior cycle closed.

## What would disqualify rapid recertification

| Disqualifier | Current position | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Material change in assessment scope | No change planned | Scope change triggers an off-cycle evaluation (09.06 §7) |
| A significant control failure or reportable breach in the period | None | Standing |
| Prior-cycle CAPs unclosed | **8 open** — the live risk | All dated before the 2027-07-15 evidence freeze; monthly reporting |
| Certificate lapse | Expiry is a Board calendar item | Backward-planned timeline in 09.09 §3 |

## Consequences

- The 2027 critical path runs through **CAP closure**, not through assessment fieldwork.
- The r2 decision has written criteria, so it will be made on evidence rather than on ambition.
- If a CAP slips past the evidence freeze, the fallback is a full i1 assessment at roughly **+$180K and +8 weeks** — quantified in advance rather than discovered late.
