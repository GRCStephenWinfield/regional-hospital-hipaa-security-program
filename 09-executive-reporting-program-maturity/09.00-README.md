## What this phase is

The closing phase. It does four things: it **reports** the eleven-month program to the Board, it **measures** what was built, it **hands the program over** to business-as-usual, and it **schedules** the obligations that outlive the project — a certificate that expires 2027-11-29, an annual evaluation under §164.308(a)(8), and a proposed rule that may or may not finalise.

It deliberately does **not** move the risk register. Reporting is not evidence. A closing phase that improved its own numbers would be re-scoring risk without new information, and the whole portfolio's credibility rests on not doing that.

## The two sentences the Board actually heard

> MercyBridge has an externally validated security program, a HITRUST i1 certificate, and a Satisfactory internal audit opinion. It **cannot yet prove it can restore its Tier 1 clinical systems at enterprise scale**, the test that would prove it is overdue, and the associated risk was **raised** rather than quietly left where it was.

Both halves were on the first page. Under **ADR-0025** that ordering is now the standing format: exceptions first, achievements last, in good years and bad.

## What this phase refuses to claim

Four statements are made explicitly, because the omission of any one of them is how a program report becomes marketing:

1. **HITRUST certification is not HIPAA compliance.** OCR neither endorses nor recognises any certification as a compliance determination, and no amount of certification changes that.
2. **Enterprise-scale restoration is unproven.** The joint vendor test slipped, the condition attached to R-23's reduction lapsed, and the risk went back up. It is the one overdue item in the tracker and it is Board-visible by rule.
3. **Every remediation is under three months old.** Time is the only test of durability, which is why ten residuals are held rather than reduced and why no maturity domain is scored above Level 4.
4. **The program was lucky as well as good.** Three low-severity incidents in eleven months is a favourable draw, not a control outcome. `09.10` says so in detail.
