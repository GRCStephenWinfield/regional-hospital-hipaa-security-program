## 2. What is accepted, stated precisely

Acceptance covers the programme **as documented and as independently tested at 2026-12-31**. It does not constitute:

- a determination of HIPAA compliance by any regulator;
- a warranty that the safeguards will prevent a breach;
- closure of the **23 open items**, each of which transfers to a named operational owner with its date intact;
- validation of enterprise-scale restoration, which **has not been demonstrated** and is the subject of the one overdue item.
