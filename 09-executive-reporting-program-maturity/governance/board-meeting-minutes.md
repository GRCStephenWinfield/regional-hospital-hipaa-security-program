# Board of Directors — Extract of Minutes

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Meeting | Board of Directors, MercyBridge Health Network |
| Date | 2026-12-18 |
| Present | Dr. Helen Marsh (CEO), Robert Feldman (Chair, Audit &amp; Compliance Committee), Daniel Cho (CISO), Karen Boyd (CCO), Anthony Ruiz (CIO), Lisa Coleman (General Counsel), Rebecca Stern (Privacy Officer), Michelle Tran (VP Risk), Priya Anand (Internal Audit), non-executive directors |
| Item | Annual HIPAA Security Program report |

## 1. Report received

The CISO presented the annual report on the HIPAA Security Program covering the period 2026-02-02 to 2026-12-31. The Board noted the programme is **externally validated rather than self-assessed**: an independent penetration test, an internal audit reporting to this Committee, and a HITRUST i1 validated assessment quality-reviewed by HITRUST.

## 2. The order of the presentation was itself discussed

A non-executive director observed that the pack opens with four red measures — one of which is the overdue item itself — before it reaches the certification. The CISO confirmed this is deliberate and is now the standing format. **The Board endorsed it.** A director noted that this is the first management pack in her tenure to lead with its worst number.

## 3. Matters recorded

| # | Matter | Board position |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | **HITRUST i1 certification achieved** at 93.1%, valid to 2027-11-29 | Noted. The Board **specifically recorded** management's statement that certification is **not** a determination of HIPAA compliance and that OCR recognises no certification as such |
| 3.2 | **M-2 — the joint restoration test — is overdue** and has been re-baselined once | **The Board declined to accept a further re-baseline.** A second slip is to be brought to the Chair with a recovery plan, not a new date. Anthony Ruiz to report monthly |
| 3.3 | **Two risks were raised** during the year on independent evidence (R-23, R-41) | Noted with approval. The Chair observed that a register which only ever falls is one the Board should stop believing |
| 3.4 | Residual position **0 High · 16 Moderate · 40 Low**, three of which sit outside stated appetite | **Accepted**, with the three out-of-appetite residuals (R-11, R-23, R-28) recorded as knowingly held with named treatment and dates |
| 3.5 | Programme maturity **3.09 of 5**, no domain at Level 5 | Noted. The Board accepted management's position that a Level 5 score at eleven months would be unearned |
| 3.6 | 2027 plan of **~$3.19M**, of which **~$1.14M requires approval** | Referred to the Finance Committee for a January decision; the funded/requested split to be preserved in that paper |
| 3.7 | The **2025 Security Rule NPRM** is proposed and not in force; 9 of 16 proposed requirements already met | Noted. Quarterly watch confirmed |

## 4. Resolutions

1. The annual §164.308(a)(8) evaluation report is **received and accepted**.
2. The residual risk position is **accepted** as stated, including the three residuals outside appetite.
3. **No second re-baseline of M-2 is authorised.**
4. The 2027 plan is referred to Finance with the funding split intact.
5. The reporting format — worst numbers first — is **adopted as standing**.
