## Conventions
`NN.NN` numbering in execution order; metadata block per document; footer navigation; Mermaid diagrams; color-coded Excel. ADRs are numbered continuously across the portfolio (Phase 09 uses 0022–0025, closing the series at 25). The keystone deliverables are the executive summary (09.01), the §164.308(a)(8) annual evaluation (09.06) — the one document in the phase that is a regulatory instrument rather than a management report — the retrospective (09.10), and the portfolio index with its full Security Rule citation map (09.12). The Excel trackers are generated by parsing the narrative documents: the metric catalogue from 09.03, the maturity scores from 09.04, the Moderate residuals from 09.05, and the sequenced plan from 09.08, with assertions on every count, so the workbooks cannot drift from the prose.
