## Summary
The Committee reviewed the **~180 business associate population** and the tiering that places **8 Critical and 16 High** associates under enhanced oversight. All 24 now hold current assurance evidence, and subcontractor disclosure moved from 15 to **24 of 24**, registering **71 downstream entities** including 9 fourth parties.

The Committee reinforced the point that carries the whole discipline: **a covered entity cannot outsource its liability.** MercyBridge remains answerable to OCR for ePHI held by an associate, which is why documentary assurance alone was judged insufficient for the Critical tier.

## Decisions
- DEC-601 four-factor tiering; DEC-604 close the three remediations.
