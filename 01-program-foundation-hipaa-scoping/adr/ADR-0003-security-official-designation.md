## Context
§164.308(a)(2) requires a covered entity to identify a single security official responsible for developing and implementing the required policies and procedures.

## Decision
Designate **Daniel Cho (CISO) as the HIPAA Security Official**, with defined authority, independence, and reporting to the CEO and the Board Audit & Compliance Committee, coordinating with the Privacy Officer.

## Alternatives Considered
**Shared/committee ownership** — rejected: the rule requires a single official. **Privacy Officer also as Security Official** — rejected: separation improves focus and coverage.

## Consequences
Clear accountability satisfying §164.308(a)(2); coordinated security/privacy governance.
